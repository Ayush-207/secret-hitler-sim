CROWN_CAP sensitivity sweep -- stats1-9g liberals, 4,000 games per cell (seed 9000+j*100000)
Question: what should the Hitler-cap be at >=3F (the checkmate zone)? Default was 0.25.

CROWN_CAP |  compliant     greedy      groom      blend  strategic | worst
     0.40 |     58.5       95.0       92.5       79.8       93.4  | 58.5
     0.25 |     58.7       95.0       92.6       79.8       93.5  | 58.7   <- old default
     0.15 |     59.5       96.2       93.8       80.0       94.7  | 59.5
     0.10 |     62.8       96.8       94.5       81.6       95.4  | 62.8   <- peak
     0.05 |     57.7       96.0       94.8       82.2       95.9  | 57.7
     0.02 |     52.3       94.5       94.1       79.9       95.1  | 52.3

Reading the curve:
- Too loose (0.40-0.25): flat. Compliant-Hitler is groomed to a median public
  P(Hitler) of 0.074 at crowning (3,000-game measurement), so no cap in this
  range stops him. The old 0.25 default was effectively inert against compliant.
- Too tight (0.05-0.02): the cap starts blocking liberal chancellors too. At
  0.02 compliant drops to 52.3 -- no government passes, chaos and tempo loss
  eat the policy race.
- The peak at 0.10 is an interior optimum: both failure modes are structural,
  so the peak's existence is robust; its exact location could shift with a
  differently-groomed Hitler doctrine (4k-game noise is ~+/-1 point, and the
  0.10 vs 0.15 gap is bigger than that, but the true peak may sit in 0.07-0.15).

This sweep motivated counter-B ("tighter crown cap"). Counter-A (zeroing worlds
where a seat was chancellor at >=3F and the game continued -- provably not
Hitler) is exact bookkeeping with no parameter. A+B together: 64.9 vs compliant
in 4k-game prototypes; see README "Calibrating the crown cap".
